## CampReady Privacy Policy
**Effective Date: June 2026**

CampReady is built as a privacy-first utility application. 

### 1. Data Collection & Transmission
- **No Personal Data Collection:** CampReady does not collect, track, or harvest any personally identifiable information, account credentials, or user locations.
- **Local On-Device Storage:** All user-created packing manifests, travel checklists, and gear data configurations are stored strictly on the user's local device. No data is synchronized or transmitted to external company databases or tracking servers.
- **Affiliate Link Tracking:** Clicking the price tag icons launches native search parameters to help users find camping gear. As an Amazon Associate, the developer earns from qualifying purchases.

### 2. Contact Support
For support queries, data deletion checks, or policy questions regarding CampReady, contact your developer support email.

## Vaultera Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

Vaultera is a local-first home inventory and asset tracking app. We do not require you to create an account to use core inventory features.

### 1. Information stored on your device

The App stores locally on your phone or tablet:

- Rooms, items, categories, estimated values, serial numbers, notes, and ownership flags
- Photos you attach to items
- App preferences such as theme
- Purchase entitlement status provided by the app stores / RevenueCat SDK on device

This data stays on your device unless you export it, share a report or backup, or use a feature that sends data off-device as described below.

### 2. Information sent off-device

- **AI Photo Scanner:** If you tap Analyze with AI, the selected image and a text prompt are sent to Google Gemini (or a comparable AI API) to generate suggested fields. Do not scan photos you do not want processed by that service.
- **Purchases:** In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and validated with RevenueCat using store purchase tokens. We do not receive your full payment card number.
- **Feedback:** If you submit Feedback in Settings, your message and email are sent to our Formspree endpoint so we can respond and improve the App.
- **Exports and backups:** If you export CSV/PDF or create a backup ZIP, you choose where to share or save those files. Those destinations are outside our control.

### 3. Analytics and advertising

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs. We do not sell your personal inventory data.

### 4. Children

Vaultera is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). Do not use the App if you are under that age.

### 5. Data retention

Local inventory remains until you delete items or uninstall the App. Feedback submissions are retained as needed to respond and improve the product. Store purchase records are retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their policies.

### 6. Your choices

- Review and delete inventory items and photos in the App
- Decline AI analysis by not using Analyze with AI
- Control backups and exports yourself
- Restore or manage purchases through your app store account
- Contact us via Feedback in Settings

### 7. Contact

For privacy questions about Vaultera, use Feedback in Settings.
